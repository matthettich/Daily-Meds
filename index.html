Welcome to Daily meds

A daily checklist for your medications, plus a simple log of how you're doing.

  • Check off your meds by time of day. A fresh list appears every morning.
  • Log symptoms, meals and mood on the Today page in a few taps.
  • Summary shows patterns over the month, like symptoms that follow certain foods or weather, to talk over with your doctor.
  • Your data stays on this phone. Nothing is uploaded. If you turn on weather, only your rough location is sent to get the forecast.
  • Save a backup now and then (Settings, Your data), especially before switching phones or clearing browser data.

Tip: install it as an app from Settings, under Personalize. This app keeps records; it doesn't give medical advice.
