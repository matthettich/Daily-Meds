Today

Today is your daily checklist. Medications are grouped by time of day: Waking, Morning, Afternoon, Evening and Bedtime. A fresh checklist appears every day.

  • Tap a medication to check it off. The time you took it shows on the right. Tap it again to uncheck it.
  • Take all checks off everything in that time of day at once.
  • The bar at the top shows how many of today's doses are checked off.
  • Use the ‹ › arrows to look at or fill in past days. Tap the date, or Back to today, to return.
  • Notes at the bottom is for anything about the day. It saves as you type.

On a wide screen, like the Fold opened up, all the pages show side by side instead of as tabs.

Meals

Each day has Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner and After dinner boxes on the Today page.

  • Add food… picks from your food list. Choose + New food… to type one that isn't there yet.
  • Choose the same food again to count another serving. It shows as Coffee ×2.
  • Tap × on a food to remove one serving.
  • Restaurant… records where you ate. Pick Not eating out to clear it.

Meds

Your medication list is in Settings, under Medications and Supplements.

  • Add a medication with its name, dose, the times of day you take it, and an optional note such as "with food". Pick every time you take it; it appears once in each.
  • Tap a medication to change it after a doctor visit, or to delete it.

Additional medications

For as-needed meds like Tylenol or aspirin that aren't taken at a set time. Add them under Additional medications with a name and dose.

  • On Today, the Additional medication box sits below your timed doses. Add medication… lists your additional meds and your regular ones, so you can log an extra dose of either.
  • Each dose is saved with the time you logged it. Take it twice and it shows twice. Tap × to remove a dose logged by mistake.
  • Logged it late? Tap the time on a dose to change it to when you actually took it.
  • Recap shows how many doses and days of each, and the export includes them.

Supplements

For vitamins, minerals and other supplements. Add them under Supplements with a name and dose.

  • On Today, the Supplements box sits below Additional medication. Pick one from Add supplement… to log it with the current time.
  • Pick the same one again and it shows ×2, ×3 and so on. The time shown is the latest one.
  • Tap the time to change it, or × to remove one. Recap and the export include supplements too.

Settings also has the Backup box. See Your data.

Symptoms

  • On Today, use Add symptom… to log a symptom for that day, or + New symptom… to create one. Tap × to remove it.
  • Each symptom shows the time you logged it. Tap the time to change it to when it actually started, or check All day if it lasted the whole day.
  • The Symptoms section of Settings lists every symptom you track and how many days you've had it this month. Tap one to rename or delete it.

Food

  • The Food section of Settings holds your lists of foods and restaurants, with how often each came up this month.
  • Tap one to rename it or delete it. Deleting a food or restaurant removes it from past meals too.
  • If you add a name that already exists, the app reuses it instead of making a duplicate.

On Today, the Medication, Symptoms, Food and Mood sections each end with their own Notes box for that day, and there's a general Notes box at the very bottom.

Mood & emotions

  • On Today, below the meals, there are Morning, Afternoon and Evening boxes. Use Add mood or emotion… to log how you felt, as many as fit. Tap × to remove one.
  • The Mood section of Settings holds your list. It starts with a few common ones; tap one to rename or delete it, or Add your own.
  • Recap shows which moods came up most, and the export has a column for each time of day.

Weather

Set a location in the Weather box in Settings, in the Recap section at the bottom. From then on the app saves each day's temperature, rain, humidity, air pressure and air quality, and fills in the past three months.

  • Recap's Weather & symptoms box compares days you had symptoms with days you didn't. Pick one symptom to narrow it down.
  • Pollen in the US needs a free Google Pollen API key. Pollen only comes as a forecast, so it's saved for each day you open the app and can't be filled in for past days.
  • Weather comes from Open-Meteo. Only your location is sent, rounded to about a kilometer.

Recap

Switch between Month and Year, and use the arrows to go back in time.

  • Top symptoms ranks symptoms by how many days you had them. Arrows compare with the same days of the previous month or year: ▲ more, ▼ fewer.
  • The Calendar shades each day by how many symptoms you logged. Tap a day to open it.
  • Medication shows the share of doses you checked off, counted from when you started using the app.

Export for AI

Downloads a spreadsheet (CSV) with one row per day: doses taken and missed, symptoms, each meal's foods and restaurant, mood, weather, air quality, pollen, and your notes. Share it with your doctor, or upload it to Claude and ask whether symptoms tend to follow certain foods, restaurants or missed doses.

Your data

Everything is stored on this phone only. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the app works without internet.

  • Save backup (in Settings, under Your data) downloads a file with all your data. Do this before switching phones, uninstalling, or clearing browser data.
  • Restore from backup loads that file. It replaces everything currently in the app.

On Android, backups and exports land in your Downloads folder. On iPhone, look in the Files app under Downloads.

Install & updates

Android (Samsung, Pixel and others)

Open the app in Chrome or Samsung Internet, open the browser menu, and tap Install app or Add to Home screen. It opens full-screen like a normal app.

iPhone

  • Open the app in Safari.
  • Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up). On newer iPhones it may be under the ⋯ menu in the address bar.
  • Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. If it's missing, tap Edit Actions at the bottom of the list and add it.
  • Turn on Open as Web App if you see it, then tap Add.

On iPhone, the home-screen app and Safari keep separate data. Use the home-screen app every day so your records stay in one place. If you start in Safari first, move your data over with Save backup and Restore from backup.

Updates

The app updates itself. Every time it starts with internet, it loads the newest version. Your records stay on the phone and aren't touched by updates.

Switching away and coming back usually doesn't count as starting, especially on iPhone, which tends to resume the copy already in memory. To get an update right away, close the app fully and open it again:

  • Android: open Recent apps, swipe the app away, then open it again.
  • iPhone: swipe up from the bottom of the screen and pause to see your open apps, swipe the app up and away, then open it again.

A new version can take a few minutes to become available after it's published, sometimes up to about 10. If it still doesn't show up, wait a bit and close and reopen the app again.

If the app misbehaves, open the check page and screenshot it.
