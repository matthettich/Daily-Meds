Today

Today is your daily checklist. Medications are grouped by time of day: Waking, Morning, Afternoon, Evening and Bedtime. A fresh checklist appears every day.

  • Tap a medication to check it off. The time you took it shows on the right. Tap it again to uncheck it.
  • Checked it off late? Tap the time on the right to change it to when you actually took it. In that pop-up (and every pop-up with a time), tap anywhere on the time to open the time picker, not just the small clock icon.
  • Take all checks off everything in that time of day at once. A medication with types gets the one you took last time.
  • Not taking a dose on purpose? Press and hold the medication and choose Skip. It counts as done, so it stops waiting to be checked (a skipped day-ending dose still ends the day), and it shows a Skipped tag. Recap counts skipped doses separately, and the spreadsheet lists them in skipped_doses rather than as taken or missed. Press and hold it again to Undo skip. On a computer, right-click instead.
  • Tap the name of anything you logged (an additional medication, a supplement, a smell, an Other entry, an appointment) to change its time, add a comment, or link it to a symptom: Tylenol → Headache, miconazole cream → Athlete's foot. The chip shows → Headache and the start of the comment. Foods do the same from their ingredients pop-up, and activities from their details pop-up. The spreadsheet adds each comment and link next to the entry, and a linked_symptoms column lists every link that day.
  • The bar at the top shows how many of today's doses are checked off.
  • Use the ‹ › arrows, or swipe across the page, to look at or fill in past days: swipe right to go back a day, left to go forward. Tap the date, or Back to today, to return.
  • Notes at the bottom is for anything about the day. It saves as you type.
  • Today runs in the order of the day's story: medications, food, smells & other, mood, symptoms, appointments, notes, then overnight & dreams. Tap a section's heading to fold it away; folded, it shows a quick count, and it stays folded until you tap it again.
  • In Settings, under Today page, untick Show … to hide a section, or use the arrows to move it up or down. Reset order puts them back in the usual order.
  • Same as yesterday? Tap a box's name (Supplements, Breakfast, Snacks & Other Meals, Morning, Symptoms, Smells, Other, Activity, Appointments, or any notes box such as Food notes or Notes) and choose Copy Yesterday's Meal (or Moods, Symptoms, Notes and so on). It copies what you logged in that box the day before, servings, ingredients, restaurant and intensity included, then asks what time it was. Undo copy in that pop-up puts the box back. Notes copy yesterday's text and don't ask a time. If the box already has something in it, it asks before replacing it. Medications, Additional medication and Medication notes don't have this, since doses are a checklist. Looking at a past day, it copies the day before that one.
  • Think something set off your symptoms? In the same menu, tick Suspected trigger. The box gets a Trigger? tag for that day, and the spreadsheet's suspected_triggers column lists the box and what was in it, so your doctor (or AI) can check whether the data backs it up. On Symptoms the checkbox is Suspected Flare instead: tick it on a day you think was a flare-up, the box gets a Flare? tag, and the spreadsheet's suspected_flare column shows a 1 for that day. Untick it to take the mark off. Don't want it? Untick Suspected triggers in Settings, under Today page; marks you've already made are kept.
  • Something changed? Every list in Settings (meds, supplements, symptoms, foods, restaurants, moods, smells, other things and appointments) has an Archive button. Archived items leave Today's lists but keep their history, and Unarchive brings them back.

Today's sections alternate down the page: white boxes with a colored title bar, then colored boxes with a white title bar. Recap and Settings do the same; to go back to their old look, untick Alternate colors on Recap and Settings in Settings, under Personalize.

On a wide screen, like the Fold opened up, all the pages show side by side instead of as tabs.

Meals

Each day has Breakfast, Lunch, Snacks & Other Meals, Dinner and After dinner boxes on the Today page. Snacks & Other Meals is for anything in between, like an afternoon snack or a second lunch. Give each food there its own time.

Don't need Snacks or After dinner? Untick Show snacks or Show after dinner in Settings, under Food. Anything already logged there is kept and still goes in the export; tick it again to bring the box back.

  • Add food… picks from your food list. Choose + New food… to type one that isn't there yet.
  • Tap the clock on a food to add when you ate it. If only one food has a time, the whole meal gets that time. Give another food its own time if it was eaten separately, or tap its time and choose Clear time.
  • Choose the same food again to count another serving. It shows as Coffee ×2. Tap its time to give each serving its own time (say, one coffee at 7:15 and another at 10:40), add another serving or remove one. It still shows as ×2, and the export lists every time.
  • Tap × on a food to remove one serving.
  • Tap a food's name to add its ingredients, a linked symptom or a comment for that meal. Choose Log once to keep the ingredients with just that meal, or Save as version to name them (like "Oatmeal · with banana"). A small dot means a meal has its own ingredients.
  • A food with types (versions), like Rice with jasmine and brown, shows once in Add food… with a ›. Picking it asks which one, or Just Rice. Add or rename types in Settings, under Food: tap the food, then + Add type. A type's details are its ingredients.
  • Restaurant… records where you ate. Pick Not eating out to clear it.
  • Had the same breakfast as yesterday? Tap Breakfast and choose Copy Yesterday's Meal, then set the time you ate.

Meds

Your medication list is in Settings, under Medications and Supplements.

  • Add a medication with its name, dose, the times of day you take it, and an optional note such as "with food". Pick every time you take it; it appears once in each.
  • Tap a medication to change it after a doctor visit, or to delete it.
  • Switch between a few? Give it types: say a medication called Antihistamine with types Allegra 180 mg, Zyrtec 10 mg and Claritin. Tap + Add type in its editor for each, with its dose in the second box. On Today, checking it off asks which one (the one you took last time is marked), and the row shows it. Tap the time to change which one it was. + New type… in that pop-up adds one on the spot. Additional medications, supplements, foods, smells, Other things and activities can have types too; picking one asks which. A type you remove that past days used is kept for history.
  • Up past midnight? Under End of day, pick the bedtime dose you take last. Until you check it off, the app stays on that day, so late doses, food and symptoms land there with their real times. If it's still unchecked at 3 AM, the new day starts. Choose At midnight to turn this off.
  • Every dose you check off keeps the time you tapped it, not just the part of the day. That's what lets Recap, the export and the Overnight section work out how long after a dose something happened. Took it earlier than you checked it? Tap the time to fix it.
  • Doctor changed something? Under Medication changes, tap Add, pick the day and write what changed, like "Started montelukast 10 mg". That day's Today page shows it above the doses (tap it to edit), the Recap calendar marks the day, and Recap's Medication changes box compares the 14 days before with the days after: days with symptoms, flare-ups, wake-ups a night and nightmares. The spreadsheet has a medication_changes column.
  • Stopped taking one? Tap Archive instead of deleting it. It leaves the checklist from that day on and stops counting as missed, but every dose you took stays in Recap and the export. Unarchive (or adding a med with the same name) puts it back, and the days it was archived still aren't counted as missed.

Additional medications

For as-needed meds like Tylenol or aspirin that aren't taken at a set time. Add them under Additional medications with a name and dose.

  • On Today, the Additional medication box sits below your timed doses. Add medication… lists your additional meds and your regular ones, so you can log an extra dose of either.
  • Each dose is saved with the time you logged it. Take it twice and it shows twice. Tap × to remove a dose logged by mistake.
  • Logged it late? Tap the time on a dose to change it to when you actually took it.
  • Recap shows how many doses and days of each, and the export includes them.

Supplements

For vitamins, minerals and other supplements. Add them under Supplements with a name and dose.

  • On Today, the Supplements box sits below Additional medication. Pick one from Add supplement… to log it with the current time.
  • Pick the same one again and it shows ×2, ×3 and so on. The time shown is the latest one.
  • Tap the time to change it, or × to remove one. Recap and the export include supplements too.

Settings also has the Backup box. See Your data.

Symptoms

  • On Today, use Add symptom… to log a symptom for that day, or + New symptom… to create one.
  • Had it again later? Pick it from Add symptom… again. Each time is saved with its own time, and it shows once as Headache ×2. Tap × to remove the latest one.
  • Tap a symptom's name to see every time you logged it that day. Set each one's intensity from 1 (mild) to 10 (severe), change its time, mark it All day, or remove it. The strongest one shows as a small number on the chip.
  • Tap the time on the chip to change the latest one to when it actually started.
  • Having a flare-up? Tick Flare-up in the Symptoms box. It saves the time it started without naming any symptom; tap the time to change it. It's the same mark as Suspected Flare in the box's menu. Recap counts flare-up days and outlines them on the calendars, and the spreadsheet has suspected_flare and flare_time columns.
  • Recap shows how many times and the average intensity, and the export has a count and the strongest intensity for each symptom.
  • The Symptoms section of Settings lists every symptom you track and how many days you've had it this month. Tap one to rename or delete it.

Food

  • The Food section of Settings holds your lists of foods and restaurants, with how often each came up this month.
  • Tap one to rename it or delete it. Deleting a food or restaurant removes it from past meals too.
  • Archive a food or restaurant to take it off the pick lists on Today without losing its history, handy for places you ate while traveling or foods you've stopped eating. Archived ones move to the bottom of the list, still show in the export, and Unarchive brings one back. Adding one with the same name brings it back too.
  • If you add a name that already exists, the app reuses it instead of making a duplicate.

On Today, the Medication, Symptoms, Food and Mood sections each end with their own Notes box for that day, and there's a general Notes box at the very bottom.

Mood & emotions

  • On Today, below the meals, there are Morning, Afternoon and Evening boxes. Use Add mood or emotion… to log how you felt, as many as fit. Felt the same thing again? Pick it again: each time gets its own time and it shows as Anxious ×2. Tap × to remove the latest one.
  • The Mood section of Settings holds your list. It starts with a few common ones; tap one to rename or delete it, or Add your own.
  • Tap the clock on a mood to add when you felt it. If only one mood in the morning, afternoon or evening has a time, the rest of that group gets it too. Tap a time and choose Clear time to remove it.
  • Recap shows which moods came up most, and the export has a column for each time of day.

Overnight & dreams

The last section on Today is for the night: wake-ups and dreams. Want it nearer the top? Move it up in Settings, under Today page.

  • Woke up? Tap + Woke up now. It's saved straight away with the time, then a pop-up asks what woke you (a dream, a symptom, nothing, not sure), what was going on in your body (racing heart, shaking, nausea, stomach or gut…), your feelings (anxious, teary, agitated…), how hard it was from 1 to 5, and a comment. All optional: Cancel keeps the wake-up with just its time, and you can fill the rest in the morning by tapping it.
  • The pop-up shows Since last dose: how long before the wake-up you last took each thing that evening, like "Cromolyn 4h 40m · Montelukast 9h 30m". The box also lists that evening's doses with their times.
  • Dreams: rate how vivid they were from 1 to 5, tick Disturbing or Nightmare, or None remembered, and jot down what it was about if you like. Tap a number again to clear it.
  • Which night? Until 6 PM the section shows last night. From 6 PM it switches to tonight, with a line about last night at the top. A past day shows the night before it, and there + Add a wake-up starts at 3 AM so you can set the time.
  • Recap's Overnight & dreams box counts nights with a wake-up, wake-ups a night, the usual time, what woke you, your feelings and body, and vivid, disturbing and nightmare nights. It counts from the first night you logged, and a night with nothing logged counts as no wake-ups.
  • The spreadsheet puts each night on the row of the evening before it, next to that evening's doses, in the night_after_… columns: how many wake-ups, each one's details and time since each dose, and the dream ratings.

Smells & Other

  • Smells are off by default. Turn on Track smells in the Smells & Other section of Settings.
  • Other is for anything else worth tracking that might matter, like air travel, a hot tub or a late night. It starts with Got overheated, for heat that isn't an activity: a warm room, a hot shower, a car in the sun. It's on by default; turn off Track other things to hide it.
  • On Today they sit below Mood. Pick one from Add smell… or Log something… to log it with the current time; tap the time to change it, or × to remove one. Pick the same one again and it shows ×2.
  • Each list lives in Settings under its switch. Recap and the export include both. Turning a switch off hides the box but keeps what you've logged.
  • A Smells & other notes box sits below them for anything else worth writing down.

Activity

  • For anything that takes effort: a walk, yoga, cleaning, errands. Add your activities in Settings, under Activity, or pick + New activity… on Today.
  • Pick one from Log activity… to log it with the current time as its start. Tap the time to change it, or × to remove one.
  • Tap an activity's name to add how many minutes it took and how hard it felt, from 1 (easy) to 10 (all-out). The highest rating shows next to the name, and the minutes on the chip.
  • Recap's Exertion & symptoms box compares harder activities (6–10) with easier ones (1–5): how often a symptom was logged during them or in the 6 hours after. Only rated activities count.
  • The export lists each activity with its time, minutes and exertion, plus a column per activity, like the symptom columns.

Appointments

  • The Appointments box is near the bottom of Today, just above Notes. Pick one from Add appointment… to log it with the current time, or + New appointment… to add one, with who or where it's with.
  • One appointment can have several people or places, like Therapy with two therapists. Add them in Settings with + Add another, or use + New appointment… with the same name and the new therapist; the ones already there are kept. Each shows up in Add appointment… as its own choice (Therapy · Dr. Lee, Therapy · Dr. Kim), and each visit keeps its own.
  • Tap a visit's practitioner or time to change either one for that visit only, or × to remove it. Seeing someone new? Choose + Add practitioner… in that pop-up: the appointment opens with a blank row, and whoever you add there goes on that visit. Removing someone in Settings keeps their name on past visits. Appointment notes below it is for what the doctor said.
  • The list lives in Settings under Appointments. To hide the section, untick Show Appointments in Settings, under Today page; what you've logged is kept.
  • Recap and the export include appointments, so you can see whether symptoms change after a visit or a new treatment.

Weather

Set a location in the Weather box in Settings, in the Recap section at the bottom. From then on the app saves each day's temperature, rain, humidity, air pressure and air quality, and fills in the past three months.

  • Recap's Weather & symptoms box compares days you had symptoms with days you didn't. Pick one symptom to narrow it down.
  • Under the location, a line shows how many of the last 30 days have weather saved, and lists any missing days. Missing days fill in by themselves the next time the app is open and online, up to three months back.
  • Pollen in the US needs a free Google Pollen API key. When you save the key, the app checks it right away and tells you in a pop-up whether it worked, with today's pollen. The line under the key keeps showing whether pollen is on. Pollen only comes as a forecast, so it's saved for each day you open the app and can't be filled in for past days.
  • Weather, air quality and elevation come from Open-Meteo (with Copernicus data), and pollen from Google Maps. Only your location is sent, rounded to about a kilometer. Pollen readings are kept for a year, as Google's terms ask; the rest of the weather stays.
  • Track location (a checkbox in the same box) saves where you are and your elevation once a day, the first time you open the app. Weather then follows you when you travel. Recap's Travel & symptoms box lists big moves (50+ miles, or 2,000+ ft up or down) and compares how often you had symptoms on the move day and the 3 days after with your other days. The app can't check while it's closed, so only days you open it get a location. Positions stay on your phone; they're in backups and the spreadsheet export.

Recap

Switch between Month and Year, and use the arrows to go back in time.

  • Top symptoms ranks symptoms by how many days you had them. Arrows compare with the same days of the previous month or year: ▲ more, ▼ fewer.
  • The Calendar shades each day by how many symptoms you logged. Tap a day to open it.
  • The Year calendar below it shows the whole year at a glance, each month in its own color, shaded the same way. Turn it off in Settings, under Recap.
  • Medication shows the share of doses you checked off, counted from when you started using the app, and how many of those were skipped.

Export for AI

Downloads a spreadsheet (CSV) with one row per day: each dose taken (with its time and type), doses missed or skipped, symptoms (how many times and how strong), each meal's foods and restaurant, mood, smells and other things, appointments, what you marked as suspected triggers and flares (with the flare-up's time), what you linked to a symptom, your comments, weather, air quality, pollen, and your notes. Share it with your doctor, or upload it to an AI assistant and ask whether symptoms tend to follow certain foods, restaurants or missed doses.

Your data

Everything is stored on this phone only, and the app works without internet. Your records are never uploaded. If you turn on weather, only your rough location is sent to get it. The privacy page has the details.

  • Save backup (in Settings, under Your data) downloads a file with all your data. Do this before switching phones, uninstalling, or clearing browser data.
  • Restore from backup loads that file. It replaces everything currently in the app.
  • Name (in Settings, under Personalize) is who the tracking is for. It shows on the Recap page and goes into spreadsheet and backup file names (and a name column in the spreadsheet), so files for different people don't get mixed up. Set it once and it stays.

On Android, backups and exports land in your Downloads folder. On iPhone, look in the Files app under Downloads.

Install & updates

Android (Samsung, Pixel and others)

Open the app in Chrome, then tap Install app in Settings, under Personalize (or choose Install app from Chrome's ⋮ menu). It opens full-screen like a normal app, named Daily meds, with its own icon.

If your home-screen icon has a small Chrome badge, or the app opens as "Meds", it was added as a shortcut rather than installed. Remove that icon, open the website in Chrome, and use Install app instead. Your records stay; they belong to the website, not the icon.

iPhone

  • Open the app in Safari.
  • Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up). On newer iPhones it may be under the ⋯ menu in the address bar.
  • Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. If it's missing, tap Edit Actions at the bottom of the list and add it.
  • Turn on Open as Web App if you see it, then tap Add.

On iPhone, the home-screen app and Safari keep separate data. Use the home-screen app every day so your records stay in one place. If you start in Safari first, move your data over with Save backup and Restore from backup.

Updates

The app updates itself. Every time it starts with internet, it loads the newest version. Your records stay on the phone and aren't touched by updates.

Switching away and coming back usually doesn't count as starting, especially on iPhone, which tends to resume the copy already in memory. To get an update right away, close the app fully and open it again:

  • Android: open Recent apps, swipe the app away, then open it again.
  • iPhone: swipe up from the bottom of the screen and pause to see your open apps, swipe the app up and away, then open it again.

A new version can take a few minutes to become available after it's published, sometimes up to about 10. If it still doesn't show up, wait a bit and close and reopen the app again.

If the app misbehaves, open the check page and screenshot it.
